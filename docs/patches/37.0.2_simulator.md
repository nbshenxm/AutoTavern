# Patch 37.0.2 in TavernGym (Nov 3, 2026)

Sources: the patch notes (hearthstone.blizzard.com, 37.0.2), the client data of build 256589
(HearthstoneJSON), and the client diff in [37.0.2.md](37.0.2.md), which covers pool cards only.

## How it went in

`tools/patch_overlay.py` compares every TavernGym card between the base build (253932) and
256589, including golden and pool-less cards, and writes [tools/overlays/37.0.2.json](../../tools/overlays/37.0.2.json):
131 cards (stats, Tiers, texts, keywords, and Trinket and Tavern spell prices, which the
client diff skips). The server-side parts of the notes are in `37.0.2_manual.json`: the pool
changes and the Dark Gift windows. Both are applied to `cards.json`, and `gen_carddb.py` applies
the overlays in order when it rebuilds from the bundle. The scripts' numbers were then changed by
hand, card by card.

## Readings of the text (assumptions, to check against logged games)

- **The Last One Standing**: "+10/+10 permanently and improve this" is read as +10/+10 more each
  Rally (golden: twice the amount).
- **Ultraviolet Ascendant**: still improves by its base (+4/+4 now) per Elemental played.
- **Stalwart Kodo**: "a different minion" is read as any minion that isn't a Stalwart Kodo.
- **Jar o' Gems**: a Blood Gem on every Quilboar after each attack by a friendly Quilboar (no
  limit per combat).
- **Murcules**: the hand minion is random (the Timewarped one picks the left-most).
- **Wand of Divination**: "banned in Quill lobbies" is read as lobbies with Quilboar.
- **Ashen Corruptor**: the Tavern's "this turn" bonus now keeps Attack and Health apart (+1/+2).

Not changed: heroes, Hero Powers, Quests, Anomalies, Buddies; the notes list none for
Battlegrounds.
