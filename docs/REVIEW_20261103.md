# Review, Nov 3: why the advisor stopped improving

Asked for after R1-R3 showed no gain. Sources: [EXPERIMENTS.md](EXPERIMENTS.md), the training
logs (office and cluster), the AutoTavern paper (arXiv 2610.33845), and the constructed advisor
(D:\GitHub\Hearthstone_AI_Advisor: its docs/review_20261031.md, experiments.md,
promotions.jsonl, Mcts.cs, ppo.py).

## Findings

**1. We can't see a real gain.** "From your positions" (vs_best_real) is 250 games, standard
error ±0.10-0.11. R1/R2/R3 (2.44 / 2.36 / 2.46) and every checkpoint since (2.15-2.54) lie
within about 2 SE of each other. Promoting the best reading picks noise (the constructed track
saw this: 54.4% on the gate's deals, 51.4% on fresh ones; AutoTavern's best-of-many snapshots:
+1.2-2.1 points on selection seeds, -0.7 on unseen ones). AutoTavern's whole climb over 23
versions was 3.40 → 2.33 places, about 0.05 a version, measured on 24,576 games a row. A
step like theirs is invisible to our check.

**2. Our training is about 1% of AutoTavern's and is never left alone.**

| | AutoTavern | Us (Oct 29 - Nov 3) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GPU-hours | ~18,600 (8 H200) | ~150 (one RTX 4080, some A100) |
| Decisions trained | ~7 billion in the delivered chain alone | ~1 billion over ~24 runs |
| One stage | 300 iterations x 1.05M decisions, fixed setup | 8-12 h windows, 20-60M decisions, flags changed each time |

Each of our experiments is about a tenth of one of their stages. With a gain per stage of 0.05
places and a check that resolves 0.2, an 8-12 hour bar mostly measures luck.

**3. The updates are tiny.** lr 1e-5, one epoch: KL per iteration 0.0005-0.001, policy loss
~0.0001. Habits learned early (never Upgrade, Freeze often) persist because an action near zero
probability is barely sampled and barely moved; the explore and self-imitation patches nudge it
(early Freezes 0.71 → 0.26 in big3open) but slowly.

**4. The lineage carries every earlier mistake.** smoke1 → entity → office2 → teachppo1 →
search2 → big2 → emb1/sig1 → solo1 → big3 (distilled) → big3open → big3hero: each run starts
from the last one under new flags. AutoTavern also chains stages, but each stage is long, has a
fixed setup and a KL anchor, and is judged on a large fixed exam.

**5. The advice is the raw network.** The plan search (8 sampled plans of the network's own
play, re-seeded copies, scored by the value head) gave no gain on late positions in the teacher
test (+0.03 ± 0.09). The constructed advisor's biggest and most repeatable gains came from
search, not from training:
- MCTS (PUCT) over the turn, with the value head scoring the End Turn leaf and hidden cards
  re-dealt at the root. Network + MCTS beat the planner 56-62%; 4,000 simulations beat 1,000
  53.8%; regret on your real positions fell from 0.55 at 4,000 simulations to 0.18 live (16,000).
- Search targets fed back into PPO (15% of games), once the search was clearly stronger than
  the network: the two promotions after plain PPO stalled.

Its network training also plateaued (17 failed gates in a row before line B). What kept it
improving was the gate, the search, and the fidelity checks.

**6. What is sound.** TavernGym's rules were audited against your logs and Bob's Buddy
([AUDIT.md](AUDIT.md)); the rebuild of your positions is fixed; the live bridge matches the
client in 96% of decisions. The simulator isn't the problem, and the audit was the floor
everything else needs.

## Is AutoTavern the wrong way?

The simulator and the network design, no. Their training recipe is right but needs about 100x
the compute we have, so copying it at our scale gives what we've seen: slow and unmeasurable
gains. The constructed advisor shows the other route on a small budget: make the decision with
search on top of the network, gate everything precisely, and train on what the search finds.

## Plan

1. **A precise gate (1 day).** Candidate and champion from the same seeds and start positions
   (your positions, fresh games, top-1% lobbies), paired by seed, sequential test, thousands of
   games, a fresh-seed re-check before anything ships. Target SE ≤ 0.03 places. A frozen
   yardstick (R2's network) on the progress page. Nothing ships without passing.
2. **A real turn search (2-3 days).** MCTS over the shop turn: the value head at End Turn, the
   random parts (Refresh, Discover, the next shop) sampled at chance points, the other warbands
   as last seen. First test: 128 / 512 / 2,000 simulations against the raw network on the gate.
   Then move regret on your logged positions against a deep search, as the constructed advisor
   does.
3. **Training on fixed setups.** One long run per setup, no flag churn, judged only by the gate;
   once the search beats the network, its targets go into PPO (expert iteration). Try a larger lr
   (3e-5) first: the KL says the policy is barely moving.
4. **Releases follow the gate.** A daily release ships advice and overlay fixes; a new network
   ships only when the gate passes.
